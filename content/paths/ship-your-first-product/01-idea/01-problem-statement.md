---
title: Write the problem, not the solution
type: build
est_minutes: 15
---
Every product that ships starts as a problem someone can name in one sentence.

Write yours in this shape:

> **[Who]** struggles to **[do what]** because **[why it's hard today]**.

Keep it under 30 words. If you can't name the "who", you don't have a product yet. You have a feature looking for a home.
